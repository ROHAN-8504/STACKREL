## The problem

Developers prototyping Indian e-commerce apps reach for FakeStoreAPI and get
dollar prices, Western product names and categories that do not match the
market they are building for. Demos look wrong to local stakeholders, and
seeded data has to be rewritten before anything can be shown.

DesiStore is a drop-in alternative: the same shape of catalogue API, with
products, prices, categories and imagery that read as Indian.
